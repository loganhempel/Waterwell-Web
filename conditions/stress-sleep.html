The approach

The Waterwell approach to sleep.

We look at how your day is actually structured — when you eat, when you move, what your evenings look like, what your sleep is doing across the night — and work on the pieces that are keeping the cycle going. Nutrition, routine and lifestyle support do a lot of the work here, and progress tends to be gradual rather than sudden. This is general wellbeing support; if you’re dealing with a diagnosed condition, that stays with your doctor and we work alongside it.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

How does the free discovery session work?

It’s a relaxed 15-minute conversation about what’s going on and how Kohei may be able to help, so you can decide whether to book an initial consultation. No pressure, no obligation.

Can I keep taking my current medication?

Yes. Naturopathic care is complementary — it works alongside your existing care. Kohei will never tell you to stop a prescribed medication without your wish to and a conversation with your doctor first.

What happens during a first consultation?

The initial consultation is 75 minutes. You complete a health questionnaire beforehand, then you talk through your history, lifestyle and diet in depth. You leave with a temporary plan to get started, and a fully personalised plan the following week.

How long until I see a change?

Every case and every individual is unique, so timeframes vary. Some people notice changes within weeks; deeper change takes longer. After your initial consultation you’ll receive a tailored Estimated Progress Timeline.

Does an online consultation work as well?

Yes — the consultation is the same in depth and length, from your own home. Kohei has over a decade of experience treating patients both in clinic and online.
