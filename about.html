My story.

Degree-qualified naturopath, founder and director of Waterwell Clinic, and someone who has lived with severe eczema.

“Eczema is not just a skin condition. I know, because I have lived with it myself.”

Before I became a naturopath, I was covered head to toe in eczema. It grew so severe that I had to resign from my job, and I spent nearly five months in bed, in constant pain from dry, weeping, itchy skin. Agonising is the only word I have for it.

During that time a naturopath took me through a long consultation that looked at my health as a whole, not just my skin. My experience is my own, and everyone’s skin is different, but it is the reason I chose this work.

Today I’m the founder and director of Waterwell Clinic, an online practice for people going through what I went through, and for parents looking after a child who is.

I hold a Bachelor of Natural Medicine and a Bachelor of Science, and I have worked with clients for more than ten years. My work is complementary: I am always happy to work alongside your GP, dermatologist or other health professionals. Always consult your GP or prescribing healthcare professional before changing any prescribed treatments.
