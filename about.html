Born in Tokyo, Japan and soon migrated to New Zealand at the age of 8, I had no idea that I would one day become a Natural Health Practitioner.

I first took interest in the human mind, with a Bachelor of Science majoring in Psychology. But soon after, I became bedridden by a chronic skin condition…

My Skin Journey

As a young child, maybe since the age of 5, I had minor eczema around my elbow creases. It was never severe, but I do remember some awful moments where I had to wear bandages, and my mum would try her best to use natural remedies and rub sea salt directly on my eczema instead of steroid creams - grateful in hindsight, but it was painful!

I would get through my teenage years without too much problem with my skin.

But in 2010, my eczema erupted all over my body, and it took over my life. I cannot tell you the amount of pain and the agony I went through - words just don’t quite capture the level of torment.

My body was covered in dry, flaky, weeping and uncontrollably itchy skin. It hurt to move my body, it hurt to even smile. I was bedridden all day, and I would stay up scratching all night, waking up with dead skin and blood all over my sheets, and the day would start again.

I was trapped in a cycle of using strong prescription treatments, which unfortunately could no longer hold off the intense itch and recurrent skin infections. I truly lost hope towards the end.

My relationship with Naturopathy

This is when everything changed for me. I was introduced to a Naturopath through a family member. It was the first time anyone had spent 90 minutes asking about every part of my health.

For the first time, someone asked why my skin was reacting, not just how to calm it down.

I immediately began my path to becoming a naturopath at the South Pacific College of Natural Medicine in Auckland, New Zealand, even before my condition had improved, and it immediately turned into a life passion of mine.

Over the next 12 months, by focusing deeply on nutrition, targeted dietary support, and restoring whole-body balance, my skin slowly settled.

3 years later I graduated with a Bachelor of Natural Medicine, and eventually created the Waterwell Clinic - a holistic skin support practice dedicated to supporting people who are feeling helpless, and guiding you to find your way back to comfort, resilience, and hope.

Since then I’ve had the pleasure of giving numerous seminars and webinars, mentoring, becoming one of the advisory naturopaths for Conquer Health and She Thrives group, and providing naturopathic care to over 1,000 people all over the world.

As of June 2026, Waterwell Clinic has officially relocated to Sydney, Australia, and I’m truly excited to grow as a practitioner and serve a wider group of people who are struggling with their skin!

Waterwell Clinic is

Derived from my last name - Iguchi. In Japanese, it means ‘The Mouth of the Well’. The water-well of a village is usually located in the centre of the village, and it’s considered as the life-source of the community.

It provides clean drinking water, supplies water for crops and livestock, and even serves as a central gathering space where villagers meet, exchange news, and build social bonds.

Waterwell Clinic was built around this image - a place where people can gather for sustenance, replenishment, education, and creating a positive ripple effect throughout the community.
