Eczema

I start by getting to know your unique experience with skin flare-ups, uncovering your individual drivers such as diet and environmental triggers.

Other areas of support:

Topical Steroid Withdrawal (TSW)

Navigating Topical Steroid Withdrawal (TSW) requires a whole-body approach, not just your skin. I focus on supporting your nervous system, immune system, skin integrity, and looking into the everyday factors that influenced your skin flare-ups in the first place. Through tailored nutrition, dietary guidance, and simple lifestyle adjustments, my goal is to support your body's natural stress response, ease general inflammation, and nurture skin resilience. While TSW is a gradual process, compassionate and considered guidance can help bring comfort and stability to your skin health.

Psoriasis

I take a close look at digestive function, liver health and daily lifestyle factors to support your body from the inside out, rather than focusing on surface skin plaques alone. Care is completely individual and targeted to support a calm, balanced immune system to help ease skin inflammation from within.

Acne

I support hormonal health, gut function, and your skin’s microbial balance, while looking into the daily dietary and lifestyle factors influencing your skin. My focus is on exploring why breakouts may be recurring, rather than just tending to the spots you can see.

Skin Infections

I look at the health factors that support a strong immune system and explore the internal environment to help your skin stay resilient. My goal is to support your body’s natural defences so it feels better equipped to maintain overall balance and the long-term wellbeing of the skin.

Rosacea

We identify and address the underlying microbial imbalance, digestive issues, dietary triggers and lifestyle factors behind your rosacea, working on the flushing and sensitivity by looking at its internal drivers, not just soothing the surface.

FAQs

Do you work with children and babies?

Absolutely. Children are far more likely to suffer from eczema than adults, so many of our clients are little ones. I guide the parents through a clear, step-by-step process to take the hassle out of their child’s skin journey.

How does the Free Discovery Session work?

It’s a relaxed 15-minute conversation to chat about your current skin issue and how I may be able to help — so you can feel confident before deciding to book an initial consultation. No pressure, no obligation.

How is a naturopath different from a dermatologist?

The primary difference lies in our scope and approach to care: targeted medical intervention versus holistic, internal support. A dermatologist is a medical specialist who provides diagnosis and medical treatments to help manage acute skin symptoms quickly. In contrast, as a naturopath, I focus on exploring the underlying factors that may be influencing your skin - such as gut health, nutrition, and lifestyle. Through tailored herbal support, nutritional guidance, and lifestyle adjustments, I work alongside your broader care team to support your body's natural balance and long-term wellness.

Can I keep taking my current medication?

Yes. Naturopathic care is complementary — it works alongside your existing care. I will never tell you to stop a prescribed medication without your desire to and a conversation with your doctor first.

Always consult your GP or prescribing healthcare professional before changing any prescribed treatments.

How long until I see results?

Everybody is unique, so there is no fixed timeframe. Because your skin and health background are individual to you, progress unfolds differently for everyone. After your initial consultation, I’ll provide you with a tailored Estimated Progress Timeline. This gives you a clear, transparent idea of what our work together will look like and what milestones we'll be aiming for along the way.

What happens during your initial consultation?

Your initial consultation is a comprehensive 75-minute session dedicated entirely to understanding your health journey. Before we meet, I’ll ask you to complete an intake questionnaire. During our time together, I will explore your health history, daily lifestyle, and dietary habits in depth to gain a clear picture of the factors influencing your wellness. By the end of our session, I’ll provide you with a starter plan so you feel supported right away. The following week, I’ll deliver your fully personalized care plan, tailored specifically to your body and long-term health goals.

What are the advantages of online consultations?

Have your consultation from the comfort of your own home — grab your favourite drink, settle on the couch, and we begin with a click. You can avoid the traffic and the drive across town, save on fuel and public-transport costs, and even cut your carbon footprint. With over a decade of working with clients in clinic and online, I can provide the same quality of care whether you’re in town or halfway across the world.

Can I see you if I’m not in Australia or New Zealand?

Yes! I see clients from all over the world via online consultations. Access to certain products or tests may vary depending on where you live, but I work with many international dispensaries and service providers to make sure everyone gets the care they need.
