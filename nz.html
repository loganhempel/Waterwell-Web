Topical Steroid Withdrawal

Psoriasis

We look at diet, digestion, stress and lifestyle together, and put together a plan that aims to support your skin day to day.

Acne

We look at the pattern of your breakouts alongside hormones, digestion, diet and skincare habits, with a plan designed to assist with managing flare-ups.

Skin infections

For fungal, bacterial or viral skin concerns that keep coming back, I look at the factors that may leave you more prone to them, and how diet and lifestyle may help support your skin.

See your GP promptly about any infection that is spreading, painful or comes with a fever.

Rosacea

We look at what tends to set off your flushing, such as food, alcohol, heat and stress, and build a plan that may help you manage it.

Do you work with children and babies?

Yes. Eczema is common in young children, so many of the people I see are little ones. I work through it with the parent as much as the child, so you always know what we are doing and why.

How does the free discovery session work?

It is a relaxed 15-minute video call about your skin and how I work, so you can decide whether an initial consultation feels right. No pressure and no obligation.

How is a naturopath different from a dermatologist?

A dermatologist diagnoses skin conditions and can prescribe medication. As a naturopath, I look at the wider picture, such as diet, digestion, sleep, stress and lifestyle, and how they may relate to your skin. My work is complementary, and I am happy to work alongside your GP or dermatologist. Always consult your GP or prescribing healthcare professional before changing any prescribed treatments.

Can I keep taking my current medication?

Yes. Naturopathic care is complementary and sits alongside the care you already have. I will never ask you to stop or change a prescribed medication. Always consult your GP or prescribing healthcare professional before changing any prescribed treatments.

How long does it take?

Everyone is different, so I do not give timelines up front. After your initial consultation I will talk you through what to expect, and we review how you are going together at each follow-up.

What happens in the first consultation?

It runs for 75 minutes by video. You fill in a health questionnaire beforehand, then we go through your history, diet and lifestyle in detail. You leave with some first steps, and your full written plan follows the next week.

What are the advantages of online consultations?

You can join from home, with no travel, no traffic and no waiting room, wherever you live.

Can I see you if I live outside Australia?

Yes. I see clients from around the world by video. Some products or tests are not available everywhere, and I will let you know what applies where you live.
