My eczema story

“Eczema is not just a skin condition. It’s an internal condition. I know, because I have suffered from it myself.”

Before I became a naturopath, I was covered head to toe in eczema. It grew so severe I had to resign from my job, and was bed-ridden for nearly five months — living on steroids and antibiotics, in constant pain from dry, weeping, itchy skin. It can only be described as agonising, torturous and hopeless.

When the steroids stopped and every medical avenue was exhausted, a naturopath took me through a consultation that changed everything. A year and a half later, through nutritional, dietary and lifestyle support, my skin was 99.9% eczema-free. That experience became my lifelong passion for natural medicine.

Today I’m the founder and director of Waterwell — a holistic skin clinic on a mission to care for people going through the same hardship I did, or to help others avoid it altogether.

The stepping stones

From flare-ups to the skin that you feel comfortable in.

No guesswork — five considered steps that take you from where you are now to skin that looks after itself.

  1. 01 Step 01 · Where we begin

    Identifying your skin condition

    Getting this right matters more than anything else — start in the wrong direction and you lose time, effort and money. Consultations, functional testing and over a decade of clinical experience pinpoint what’s driving it.

  2. 02 Step 02

    Removing the obstacles

    Food and environmental sensitivities are like speed bumps — they quietly fuel inflammation, leaving little room to heal. Reducing them first is what makes the rest of the plan worth doing.

  3. 03 Step 03

    Treating the underlying cause

    Once inflammation settles, we address the root of your condition — permanently avoiding allergens isn’t solving anything. We find what caused those sensitivities, using natural remedies that support gut function, hormones, detox pathways and the microbiome.

  4. 04 Step 04

    Reintroduction

    True health isn’t avoiding your triggers — it’s being strong enough to tolerate them. Once symptoms have eased and the cause is addressed, we gently reintroduce those foods and habits.

  5. 05 Step 05

    Maintenance

    Your journey isn’t over when treatment ends — some say that’s where it truly begins. You’ll use what you’ve learned to hold your progress, with a maintenance plan and occasional check-ins.
